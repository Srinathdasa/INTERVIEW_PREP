Tips

Are you also not getting interview calls?

This is very common now; every other developer is facing the same issue, whether you are immediate joiner or serving notice period.

And yes, why so few interview calls? The market and AI are part of it, but first check your ATS score — it is a very important aspect now.

Before even starting interview preparation, work on a good ATS score.

Most companies use ATS/AI tools to filter resumes based on job descriptions. If your CV doesn’t have the right keywords, it may never reach a recruiter.

What you should do:

  • Match your resume with the job description
  • Add relevant keywords based on your role

For example, if you are an Angular developer, your CV should include terms like:

Angular, TypeScript, API Integration, RxJS, NgRx (state management), Routing, Interceptors, Authentication, Performance Optimization, Build, Responsive, Cross browser, Agile, Sprint Planning, Scrum Call

  • Keep your profile 100% complete on job portals
  • Use any ATS checker or AI tool to evaluate your resume
  • Upload CV to AI tools and ask to check ATS score. If it's not correct, it provides suggestions too.

Also, even after having a good ATS score, there is no guarantee you will receive calls because only immediate or short notice period candidates are getting more calls.
